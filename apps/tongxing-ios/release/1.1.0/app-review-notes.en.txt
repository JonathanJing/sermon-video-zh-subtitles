Tongxing 1.1.0 (45) is an independent sermon listening and reading companion for iPhone and iPad. No sign-in, demo account, subscription, or in-app purchase is required.

Connect to the internet on first launch to load the published sermon catalog. For this review, select the September 27, 2026 sermon using the calendar control. The catalog currently provides Simplified Chinese, Korean, and Spanish editions for that sermon. Open the content-language control, choose an available edition, and wait for its verified audio to load. Tap Play in the playback bar, scroll the transcript, or tap a transcript time to seek. Use Watch Full Video to open the complete original English recording in the app. Availability may differ for other dates.
Test video stream: https://ai-for-god-sermon-audio.web.app/pages/2026-09-27-weekend-sermon-drive-530/full-video-browser.mp4

The interface supports Simplified Chinese, English, Korean, Spanish, and Vietnamese, plus a system-language preference. The interface-language control and content-language control serve different purposes. Changing interface language does not translate sermon text or switch audio. Content and audio come from the published catalog. Text-only editions do not play another language automatically.

Audio playback supports background listening and system media controls. Live Activities display the current audio playback state and time on supported devices. They do not use push notifications or continuously track a live venue. Tracks with an explicit download option can be downloaded in the foreground and played offline after completion. Do not assume that every catalog page provides a separate offline-download workflow.

Sound alignment is optional and available only for supported tracks with reference fingerprints. Play the exact corresponding original English recording at normal speed on another device, then start sound alignment. The app pauses target-language audio, requests microphone permission, and captures approximately 8 or 10 seconds, depending on the reference format. PCM stays in device memory and is not saved as a recording or uploaded. Cancellation or backgrounding stops capture. It is not speech recognition, semantic matching, or continuous live tracking. Unreliable matches do not seek; manual seeking remains available. A reliable match preserves the user's prior playing or paused state.

In the app's options, open Privacy and Support to view the optional anonymous usage-statistics toggle. It is disabled by default. When enabled, the app sends interface-language visits, the selected sermon and content language, actual audio language, listening seconds, and played ranges to the project's backend. A local random identifier changes each Los Angeles calendar day; the server stores a date-and-platform-specific hash for daily deduplication. There is no advertising identifier, device fingerprint, third-party analytics SDK, or cross-app advertising tracking. Records expire after 30 days; cloud deletion is asynchronous. Disabling stops new reporting and attempts to retract records from the current app run, which may fail offline. No persistent offline upload queue is used.
