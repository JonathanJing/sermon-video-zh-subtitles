## 中文

### 适用范围

本说明适用于同行原生 iPhone / iPad App。同行是独立个人证道听读项目，与 Mariners Church 无隶属、运营或背书关系。原生 App 不提供登录、广告或业务统计上传。你主动打开的原视频、网页版、Apple 或其他外部网站有各自的隐私规则。

### 短时麦克风

只有你点击听音对齐时，App 才请求麦克风权限并采集约 8 秒声音，用于在同一英文录音中查找播放位置。声音仅在设备内存中处理，不保存为录音文件、不上传、不发送至云端识别或翻译服务。取消或进入后台会停止采集。拒绝或在系统设置中撤回权限后，你仍可手动收听和定位。

### 内容下载与服务提供方

App 通过 HTTPS 下载证道目录、音频与参考指纹，内容由 Google Firebase Hosting 提供。Google 会处理请求 IP，用于检测滥用和分析服务使用情况；其公开说明将 IP 数据保留期描述为数月。此处不承诺本项目可以缩短或删除 Google 自身的服务日志。详情见 [Firebase 隐私与数据处理说明](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)。

### 本机资料与删除

音频、目录、参考指纹、语言偏好和播放历史保存在设备本机。播放历史最多 12 条；读取或更新时淘汰超过 30 天的记录，不是持续运行的定时删除服务。下载音频排除系统备份，其他资料可能依你的系统备份设置保存。删除 App 可删除当前设备的本机 App 资料；系统备份另在设备设置中管理。当前 App 不提供账户，也无账户删除流程。

### 系统播放展示

系统媒体控件、锁屏与实时活动可能显示证道标题、讲员、中文音频进度和播放状态。实时活动不使用推送服务，App 不另行上传这些状态到业务服务器。你可在系统中管理麦克风和实时活动权限。

### TestFlight 测试资料

使用 TestFlight 时，Apple 自动收集崩溃及使用信息并提供给开发者；你提交的文字或截图反馈也会被共享，可能包含姓名、邮箱、设备和构建资料，具体取决于邀请及反馈方式。Apple 表示保留 Beta 反馈一年，崩溃及使用数据可保留至问题解决。详情见 [TestFlight 与隐私](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/data/en/test-flight/)。

开发者仅将收到的测试资料用于问题排查、回复及 App 改进，不向第三方分享。开发者另行保存的可识别反馈副本在相关问题解决后 90 天内删除，仍需跟进或依法必须保留的情况除外。该期限不改变 Apple 的平台保留规则。请勿附带私人对话、凭据或无关个人资料。

## English

### Scope

This notice covers the native Tongxing app for iPhone and iPad. Tongxing is an independent personal sermon listening project, unaffiliated with and not operated or endorsed by Mariners Church. The native app has no sign-in, advertising, or business analytics uploads. Original videos, the web app, Apple services, and other external websites you choose to open have their own privacy practices.

### Microphone

Only when you start sound alignment does the app request microphone permission and capture about 8 seconds of audio to locate a position in the same original English recording. Audio stays in device memory; it is not saved as a recording, uploaded, or sent to cloud transcription or translation services. Cancelling or backgrounding stops capture. You can listen and seek manually if permission is denied or revoked in Settings.

### Content delivery

The app downloads catalogs, audio, and reference fingerprints over HTTPS through Google Firebase Hosting. Google processes request IP addresses to detect abuse and analyze service usage, and states that Hosting retains IP data for a few months. This notice does not promise that the project can shorten or delete Google's own service logs. See [Firebase privacy and data processing](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy).

### Local storage and deletion

Audio, catalogs, reference fingerprints, language preferences, and listening history are stored locally. Up to 12 history entries are retained; entries older than 30 days are discarded when history is read or updated, rather than by a continuously running deletion timer. Downloaded audio is excluded from system backups; other local data may be backed up according to your settings. Deleting the app removes its local data on that device. Manage system backups separately in Settings. The app has no accounts or account deletion workflow.

### System playback display

System media controls, the Lock Screen, and Live Activities may show the sermon title, speaker, Chinese audio progress, and playback state. Live Activities do not use push notifications, and the app does not separately upload these states to a business server. Microphone and Live Activity permissions can be managed in system settings.

### TestFlight

Apple automatically collects crash and usage information when you use TestFlight and shares it with the developer. Feedback you submit, including text and screenshots, is also shared and may include your name, email, device, and build details depending on the invitation and feedback method. Apple states that beta feedback is retained for one year and that crash and usage data may be retained until bugs are resolved. See [TestFlight & Privacy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/data/en/test-flight/).

The developer uses test data only for troubleshooting, responses, and app improvement and does not share it with third parties. Identifiable feedback copies separately retained by the developer will be deleted within 90 days after the related issue is resolved, except when continued follow-up or a legal obligation requires retention. This does not change Apple's retention rules. Do not submit private conversations, credentials, or unrelated personal information.
