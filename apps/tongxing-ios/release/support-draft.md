## 使用帮助

- 首次使用需联网读取证道目录。通过日历按钮选择周次，在“更多选项”选择音频版本。
- 点击底部播放按钮开始收听。全文中的时间按钮可定位；“定位 / 精调”支持输入时间及小幅调整。
- 下载时保持 App 在前台；下载完整后再断网使用。如果出现“使用离线版”，点击后切换到下载音轨。
- “界面语言”只切换按钮和说明，不更换音轨或翻译正文；英文原文仅在目录提供对应来源时显示。
- 听音对齐只支持同一英文录音正常 1 倍速重放，需当前音轨有配套指纹和同步资料。请用另一设备播放英文原录音，再主动点击听音对齐。它不是现场 ASR 或持续跟踪服务。
- 对齐不可用或匹配失败时，继续使用时间定位；拒绝麦克风权限不影响普通播放。已授权仍不能收音时，在系统设置中检查权限。
- 切后台会取消短时收音；正常已开始的音频播放可使用系统后台音频。耳机断开会暂停，切换路由或来电后请检查实际播放状态。
- 实时活动由系统和用户设置决定。不出现灵动岛不会阻止普通播放；App 不用实时活动来保证后台执行。

## English help

Connect to the internet on first launch. Choose a sermon with the calendar button and an audio version under More. Tap Play in the bottom bar. Use transcript time buttons or Seek / Fine Tune to adjust position.

Keep the app in the foreground while downloading. Wait for completion, and choose the offline version if offered before disconnecting. Interface language changes controls only; it does not change the audio or translate content. Original English text appears only when a matching source block is available.

Sound alignment is optional and supports the same original English recording played at normal speed, only for tracks with compatible reference data. Play that recording on another device and start alignment explicitly. This is not live transcription or continuous tracking. Manual playback and seeking remain available if matching fails or microphone permission is denied. Backgrounding cancels microphone capture. Normal ongoing playback uses system background audio.

TestFlight testers can send feedback through TestFlight. Include app and system versions, sermon date, track, time of the issue, and steps to reproduce it. Do not submit private recordings, credentials, or unrelated personal information.
